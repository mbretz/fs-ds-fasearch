# Motion

Basic strategy on motion, or animation, based on empirical research.

> An update to Motion Foundations is coming Fall 2025.

## Purpose

Use motion to:

- Maintain a user's context when transitioning state change.
- Guide the user and provide feedback appropriate to their emotional state.
- Give the impression of enhanced performance by hiding perceived latency.
- Isolate one item in a series of elements for focus.
- Use animation in a way that is WCAG AA compliant, such as avoiding rapid flashes and ensuring animations are not required for understanding.

## Do

Ensure motion is:

- Supportive of application usability.
- An extension of the brand.
- Embedded throughout the design system.
- Emotive when appropriate.
- Cohesive.
- Natural looking and reminiscent of movement in the physical world.

## Don't

- Don't use motion as a replacement for good UX.
- Don't use cliche or off-putting motion.
- Don't create distracting animations that compete with workflow.
